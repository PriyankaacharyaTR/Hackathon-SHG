## 1. One-line pitch
A voice-friendly production layer that takes a women's SHG from a bulk order to an approved, explainable payout: price it right, plan it, divide it fairly, track quality, and let every member hear how her pay was worked out.

## 2. Problem facts (from the problem statement)
- Members underprice by 30–40% because material, labour and margin are not calculated.
- Work is divided verbally, tracked in a notebook, quality checked only at the end, payment split by estimate.
- Scale: 10.08 crore women in 92.30 lakh SHGs under DAY-NRLM (PIB, Oct 2026); women SHGs are 10–20 members under government norms (RBI master circular). Re-verify citations before the final deck.

## 3. Positioning
Complements existing savings, meeting and bookkeeping tools and marketplaces; focuses on **production coordination and work-linked payouts**. Re-check for competitors before claiming none exists.

## 4. Business model
| Option | Payer | Role |
|--------|-------|------|
| Primary: federation licence | SHG federations, livelihood-mission programmes, NGOs | Core revenue |
| Upside: order fee | Buyers or SHG | Small fee per fulfilled order |
| Upside: sponsored deployment | CSR and corporate bulk buyers | Dependable women-led supply |

## 5. Pilot success metrics
Time to create plan and allocate; share of tasks on time; delays identified before delivery; rework rate and stage detected; estimated vs actual cost gap; payouts explainable from records and number of disputes; leader and member satisfaction after one order.
